## 面经正文

货拉拉全英文面试，全程10分钟就问了这些
1. Brief self-introduction — he said do NOT include any project details, just summarize what you did in your last internship in one or two sentences.
（自我介绍——明确说了不要讲项目细节，一两句话概括最近实习就行）
2. How long can you commit to this internship?
（你能实习多久？）
3. What are the controller layer and the service layer in a Spring Boot project?
4. How do you work with the frontend team? How do you write the HTTP requests?
5. Follow-up: then what about the service layer? Where do you put your core business logic?
（追问：那 service 层是干什么的？）
6. What is CRUD in MySQL?
7. Based on your own system, give an example of how you used CRUD operations.
8. Small talk: Which city are you in now? Do you need to go back to Hong Kong for classes? How many classes do you have per week?
9. Last one: What do you know about our company?
全程没碰任何八股
